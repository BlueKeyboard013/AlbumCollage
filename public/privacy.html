Privacy Policy

Album Collage lets you build a collage of your favorite album covers using your Spotify listening data. This page explains what data the app accesses and how it's handled.

What we access

When you log in with Spotify, the app requests the user-top-read scope only. This lets it read your top tracks and, if you search for a song, look up track and album details via Spotify's search endpoint. The app does not request access to your playlists, your library, your playback, or any other part of your Spotify account.

What we store

The app does not use a database and does not store your Spotify data on our servers. Your Spotify access and refresh tokens are kept only in an httpOnly cookie in your own browser, used solely to make API requests to Spotify on your behalf while you're using the app. Album art, track names, and artist names you see in the collage are fetched from Spotify on demand and are not saved anywhere by us.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share, or transmit your Spotify data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising or analytics. We do not retain your data after you log out — logging out clears your session cookies immediately.

Collages you create

Collages you build are rendered entirely in your browser. Downloading a collage as an image happens locally on your device; we never receive a copy of the image you export.

Revoking access

You can revoke this app's access to your Spotify account at any time from your Spotify account's app access settings, or by clicking "Logout" in the app, which clears your local session.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's maintainer via the contact information provided in the Spotify Developer Dashboard listing for this app.
